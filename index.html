Every prompt uses the same five parts taught in the training: Role, Context, Task, Output and Safety checks. Each comes with generated fictional material to test it. Is fictional data the only way to test? No, but it is the safest start, and the right choice for videos, slides and this website. Move up the ladder only with approval:
1. Fictional (invented, as here) · 2. Realistic synthetic (invented, but modelled on the messiness you see, such as typos, mixed date formats and blanks, without copying any real record) · 3. De-identified or aggregated real data (only with your organisation’s approval; removing names alone is not enough, as dates, places and rare details can still identify people) · 4. Real data in an approved tool (authorised tool, authorised use, human review). Fictional data cannot show every real-world problem, so judge a prompt on realistic messy samples before trusting it at work.

3. Build artifacts for complex tasks

An artifact is a small working tool, such as a checker, form or dashboard, that AI builds for you. In Claude, ask it to “build an interactive artifact”. In ChatGPT, try canvas or ask for a spreadsheet or web page where your plan offers it. Use invented data only.

1. PlanAsk the AI to outline the steps first.
2. DraftBuild one step at a time.
3. CheckTest with fictional data; try to break it.
4. RefineAsk for one change per message.

Each artifact prompt also follows Role, Context, Task, Output and Safety checks, and uses the matching fictional sample above (download it from that prompt’s card).

4. The Responsible AI checklist

Ask these eight questions before using AI for any hospital task.

1. Purpose
Is AI appropriate for this task?
2. Permission
Is this tool and use authorised?
3. Privacy
Am I protecting personal and confidential information?
4. Accuracy
How will I verify the output?
5. Risk
Could an error harm a patient or affect someone’s rights?
6. Oversight
Who must review or approve the result?
7. Security
Am I following the required security procedures?
8. Accountability
Can I explain and justify the final decision?
STOP and escalate if: the task involves unauthorised confidential information; the tool is not approved for the intended use; a high-risk clinical decision has no appropriate safeguards; or the output looks unsafe, biased, unsupported or inconsistent with authoritative information.
Never sacrifice confidentiality, accuracy, or patient safety for speed. Asking AI to check its own answer is not proof that it is correct.
